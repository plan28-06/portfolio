Projects

Scientific Claim Verification Pipeline

Developed a scientific claim-verification pipeline combining DeBERTa NLI and open-source LLMs with an agreement-based fusion strategy. Implemented hybrid evidence retrieval using Sentence Transformers and BM25, improving MRR and mean Recall over standalone approaches.

Evaluated three NLI evidence aggregation strategies on the SciFact development split; relevance-weighted aggregation performed best (0.724 macro-F1 versus 0.645 and 0.571). Constructed a 2,035-claim benchmark from SciFact and CliniFact to compare NLI, LLM, and hybrid approaches and analyze their errors. Standalone LLMs (best: Qwen3 8B, 0.79 macro-F1) substantially outperformed DeBERTa NLI (0.32 macro-F1), while the best agreement-based hybrid (0.74 macro-F1) did not surpass its standalone LLM counterpart because of frequent disagreements.

Python · DeBERTa · Sentence Transformers · BM25 · LLMs
